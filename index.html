FAQ

Technical & Installation Insights

Direct technical answers for architects, civil contractors, and property owners.

Can an elevator be retrofitted in an existing house without a concrete shaft? +
Yes. ERIS-NEXA installs self-supporting steel framework structures with panoramic glass or cladded metal through existing stair voids, atriums, or exterior facades without requiring concrete shafts.
What is a Machine Room-Less (MRL) elevator and why is it superior? +
Machine Room-Less (MRL) elevators place the gearless PMSM motor inside the hoistway, eliminating rooftop machine rooms, saving space, and cutting power consumption by up to 40%.
What happens during a sudden municipal power outage while riding? +
The Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) deploys dedicated backup batteries within seconds, smoothly leveling the car at the nearest floor and opening doors for safe passenger egress.
What pit depth and overhead height are required for villa lifts? +
Residential villa elevators require only 250–350 mm pit depth and 3200–3400 mm overhead clearance, easily fitting standard residential ceiling heights.
How fast is ERIS-NEXA's emergency breakdown response in Gurgaon? +
With our headquarters and parts warehouse at Sushant Lok, Gurgaon, our rapid technicians reach any Gurgaon site within 30 minutes 24/7.
