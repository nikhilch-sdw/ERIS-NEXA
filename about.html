ABOUT ERIS-NEXA

German Precision. Italian Luxury. Engineered to Elevate.

At ERIS-NEXA Elevators, we bring together German engineering rigor and Italian architectural aesthetics. Headquartered in Gurugram, we deliver premier vertical transportation systems tailored for bespoke villas, luxury penthouses, commercial towers, and healthcare campuses across Delhi NCR and India.

With an uncompromising focus on passenger safety, energy-saving PMSM gearless traction, and precision CAD shaft planning, we provide turnkey mobility solutions that perform with absolute consistency, whisper-quietness, and lasting peace of mind.
