Landmark Project Case Studies

How ERIS-NEXA engineers tailored civil hoistway designs to match architectural visions across challenging NCR sites.

CASE STUDY 01 • RESIDENTIAL VILLA

DLF Phase 1: Zero Pit Depth Civil Constraint

Challenge: An existing 4-story luxury residence had no pre-cast elevator shaft and underground foundation restrictions prevented excavating a standard 1,400 mm pit.

ERIS-NEXA Solution: Engineered a bespoke MRL Home Lift requiring only 300 mm pit depth using a cantilever car frame and structural glass steel tower anchored to existing slab beams.

300 mm Pit 230V Single Phase Titanium Gold Finish
CASE STUDY 02 • CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS

Golf Course Road: High-Traffic Surge Optimization

Challenge: 12-story commercial tower experiencing severe morning lobby crowding with wait times exceeding 90 seconds.

ERIS-NEXA Solution: Modernized existing hoistways with dual 1.75 m/s PMSM gearless machines and integrated dynamic dispatch algorithms, reducing average passenger wait time to under 22 seconds.
