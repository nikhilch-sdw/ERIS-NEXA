⚙️

PMSM Synchronous Motors

High-flux neodymium rare-earth magnets deliver superior torque without traditional worm gearboxes. Eliminates oil changes, eliminates thermal dissipation loss, and saves up to 40% on annual electricity consumption.

⚡

32-Bit VVVF Dual-Core Control

High-speed digital signal processor executes micro-second vector calculations. Calculates jerk-free S-curve velocity trajectories for unnoticeable start, travel, and millimetric landing floor leveling.

🔇

<48 dB Acoustic Level

Precision balanced sheaves and acoustic vibration isolators maintain shaft noise levels quieter than a luxury private library. Ideal for residential penthouses, master bedrooms, and executive boardrooms.

🌱

Regenerative Grid Return

During heavily-loaded descent or lightly-loaded ascent, the motor functions as a clean generator, converting gravitational potential energy into filtered AC power and returning it to the building electrical grid.
