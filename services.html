How Our Rapid Response Operates

When an elevator experiences a fault, every minute counts. Our dedicated dispatch network ensures rapid passenger rescue and swift mechanical restoration.

01

Emergency Hotline Trigger

Calls to +91 95577 61198 connect instantly to our 24/7 Gurgaon control room. The incident is logged, mapped by GPS, and assigned to the nearest patrol vehicle.

02

Sub-30 Min Site Arrival

Mobile service technicians stationed in DLF, Cyber City, Sohna Road, and Golf Course Road reach your premises in under 30 minutes equipped with diagnostic tools.

03

Safe Passenger Evacuation

Immediate priority is passenger comfort and safety using automated ARD or manual brake release procedures executed in strict accordance with IS 14665 standards.

Diagnostic & Restoration
