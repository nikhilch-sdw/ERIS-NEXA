Our bespoke Machine-Room-Less (MRL) Home & Villa elevators require a minimum pit depth of just 300 mm (or even flush floor level with a subtle 50 mm ramp where foundation excavation is impossible). Standard commercial passenger elevators require a standard 1,400 mm pit in accordance with IS 14665.
Yes, absolutely. We specialize in self-supporting architectural steel towers clad in toughened laminated glass or aluminum composite panels. These can be integrated inside an existing stairwell atrium, cut through slab openings, or erected as an exterior scenic elevator anchored to external beams.
Every ERIS-NEXA elevator includes an automated battery-backed ARD system. If grid power is interrupted, the micro-inverter immediately takes over within 3 seconds, senses the lighter-load direction (up or down), smoothly travels to the nearest landing, levels flush with the threshold, and powers open the doors so passengers walk out safely.
For our residential villa elevators (300 to 450 kg), our PMSM drives operate on a standard Single-Phase 230V, 50Hz domestic power line and consume less power than a 1.5-ton split air conditioner. For commercial passenger, freight, and automobile elevators, a standard Three-Phase 415V, 50Hz industrial connection is used.
Custom manufacturing, laser cutting, and drive assembly take approximately 3 to 4 weeks from approval of civil shaft blueprints. Mechanical erection, rail alignment, and electrical commissioning on site typically take 10 to 14 days, followed by safety load testing and handover.
