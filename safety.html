Zero-Failure Philosophy — 8 Critical Mechanisms

Multi-tiered mechanical, electrical, and electronic safety interlocks engineered strictly to IS 14665 and EN 81-20/50 international standards for absolute passenger peace of mind.

⚡

ARD (Automatic Rescue Device)

Microprocessor-controlled battery backup system that instantly detects main power grid outages, automatically navigates the elevator to the nearest landing floor, and unlocks doors for safe passenger exit.

Zero Entrapment Guarantee
🛡️

Bi-Directional Overspeed Governor

Precision centrifugal mechanical governor that continuously monitors shaft velocity. If speed exceeds 115% of rated limit in either direction, mechanical wedges bite into the guide rails to halt the car instantly.

Fail-Safe Mechanical Wedge
👁️

154-Beam Infrared Light Curtain

Full-height dense infrared optical curtain covering the door opening from top to bottom. Senses tiny obstacles or pets within 10 milliseconds and triggers an instant, touchless door reopening.

Anti-Pinch Instant Reversal
🔩

Heavy-Duty Hydraulic Pit Buffers

Precision calibrated oil-hydraulic damping buffers mounted at the shaft bottom. Provides gentle, controlled energy dissipation in the extreme event of terminal floor overtravel.

Certified Energy Dissipation
⚖️

Electronic Overload Protection

High-sensitivity strain-gauge load cells beneath the car platform that sound acoustic warnings, illuminate cabin overload indicators, and prevent the drive motor from starting until weight is reduced.

Acoustic & Optical Buzzer
🚒

Fireman Emergency Return Drive

Automated building fire-alarm BMS integration. Upon alarm activation, immediately cancels all landing calls, navigates straight to the designated evacuation level, and holds doors open for rescue personnel.

NBC Fire Code Compliant
🔌

Phase Failure & Inversion Relay

Solid-state 3-phase electrical monitoring relays guarding against phase reversal, under-voltage, and brownouts to protect motor windings from burning and eliminate reverse travel risks.

Motor Burnout Protection
📡

IoT RMS Telemetry & Intercom

Real-time remote monitoring system streaming operational telemetry and error codes directly to our central Gurgaon engineering hub, backed by a hands-free 2-way emergency intercom.

24/7 Tele-Diagnostic Monitoring

Every ERIS-NEXA elevator manufactured and erected undergoes mandatory multi-point safety testing prior to commercial commissioning.

Bureau of Indian Standards safety guidelines for electric traction passenger, goods, and hospital elevators.

European safety rules for the construction and installation of lifts and lift components.

ISO 9001:2015

International quality management certification guaranteeing rigorous fabrication and testing procedures.

National Building Code (NBC)
